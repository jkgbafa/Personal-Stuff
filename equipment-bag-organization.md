## PACKING NOTES

1. **NPF Batteries dispersed** across Bags 1, 2, 5, and 6 — never all in one bag
2. **Main camera uses Power Base, not NPF directly** — Power Base + USBC cables in Bag 1
3. **Existing phone bag** → becomes Bag 3. Relabel it.
4. **Existing transmitter bag** (4 pairs) → split: 2 stay in Bag 1, 2 go to Bag 2, rest distributed
5. **Starlink** carried separately as backup internet
6. **Tripods** carried loose or in a separate case
7. **BUY items** go into Bag 13 (Miscellaneous) once purchased
