1. **NPF Batteries MUST be dispersed** — never put all 16 in one bag (per your spreadsheet note). They're spread across Bags 1, 5, 6, 7, and 12.
2. **Main camera uses Power Base, not NPF directly** — Power Base + USBC cables are in Bag 1 with the camera.
3. **Existing bags:** Your current phone bag (4 phones) = Bag 2. Your current transmitter bag (4 pairs) = Bag 3. Relabel them.
4. **3 loose DJI TX/RX pairs** go into Bag 4.
5. **Starlink** is carried separately — it's the backup internet if Peplink/GL/LiveU in Bag 9 don't arrive.
6. **Aerotrunk cubes compress** — zip down after packing to save space in the suitcase.
7. **Tripods** (6 total including supplementary) — bundle and carry outside bags or in a separate tripod case.
