## 1. Finding Companies Hiring Receptionists/Virtual Assistants on LinkedIn

### LinkedIn Search Strategies

**Direct Job Search Keywords:**
- "virtual receptionist," "virtual assistant," "remote receptionist," "front desk"
- Filter by Remote work type, Entry Level / Associate experience

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator (Advanced):**
- **Boolean Search:** Craft strings like `"receptionist" OR "front desk" OR "virtual assistant"` to find companies actively posting these roles
- **Account Filters:** Target industries with high front-desk needs: healthcare, legal, dental, hospitality, real estate
- **Company Size:** Focus on SMBs (10-200 employees) -- these feel receptionist costs the most and are prime AI replacement candidates
- **Job Posting Signals:** Companies actively posting receptionist/front desk openings have immediate need
- **Buyer Intent Signals:** AI-powered filter that surfaces leads showing buying patterns based on platform behavior (available on Advanced/Advanced Plus plans)
- **Technologies Used Filter:** Find companies using competitor products you want to replace
- **Spotlight Filters:** Behavioral/activity-based filters that reveal leads most likely to reply

**Practical Targeting for AI Receptionist Sales:**
1. Search for companies posting receptionist job openings (these have budget + immediate need)
2. Target industries: medical/dental offices, law firms, real estate agencies, salons/spas, hospitality
3. Filter to decision-makers: Office Manager, Operations Manager, Practice Manager, Owner
4. Use keyword filters on company Overview/Specialties fields
5. Stack filters to narrow from thousands to the exact 1-5% worth contacting

**Key Companies Already in the VA/Receptionist Space:**
- Smith.ai, BELAY, MyOutDesk, Wishup, MedVA

**Market Context:**
- 49,000+ VA jobs on LinkedIn currently
- VA industry projected at $19.5B in 2025, growing to $55.4B by 2035 (CAGR 11%)
- Virtual receptionist pricing: $29/month (AI tools) to $5,000/month (premium human services) -- your AI product fits right in this gap

## 3. Cold Outreach Best Practices for AI Services

### Key Strategies

**1. Warm Up Before Pitching**
- Engage with prospects' content before sending messages
- View profiles, like posts, comment on their content
- This dramatically improves acceptance and reply rates

**2. Lead with Relevance, Not Features**
- Distill your AI service into 2-3 simple outcomes
- Translate each into a benefit that matters to the prospect
- Example: "We help dental offices save $3,000/month by replacing their front desk phone system with AI" (not "We have an AI receptionist with NLP capabilities")

**3. Use Trigger-Based Outreach**
- Target when decision-makers change roles (3x reply rates)
- New funding rounds, executive hires, tech stack changes
- Companies posting receptionist job openings = immediate trigger

**4. Multi-Channel Approach**
- LinkedIn + Email combined outreach
- 90% of outbound goes to email; LinkedIn is underutilized -- this is an opportunity
- Supplement with regular content posts to build trust

**5. Balance Automation with Human Touch**
- Don't over-automate -- prospects can tell
- Use AI for research and initial personalization, but add genuine human elements

**6. Protect Your Domain**
- Set up separate domain for cold email
- Configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC
- Warm up mailbox for 2-4 weeks before scaling

### Benchmarks
- **Connection Rate:** Aim for 30-50% accepted
- **Reply Rate:** Good = 10%+, Great = 20-25%
- **Cold Email Reply Rate:** Average is only 1-5% (video outreach can 10x this)
- **Conversion Rate:** Track % of responses that become meetings

### Cost Estimates (per 1,000-contact campaign)
- Contact search (Sales Navigator + Apollo): ~$500-800
- Message personalization (in-house): ~33 hours at $25/hr = ~$825
- Total: ~$1,300-1,600 per 1,000 contacts (in-house)

## 4. Loom Video Outreach -- Faceless vs. Face-on-Camera

### Does Video Outreach Work?
**Absolutely yes.** The data is overwhelming:
- Qwilr: Conversions jumped from 28.6% to 57% with Loom videos
- Intercom: Cold email reply rates +19%, $120K in outbound deals
- SalesLoft: +16% open rates, +26% replies, 4x click-through
- Forrester: 300% increase in click-through rate with video in email
- Zendesk: 41.6% response rate with video vs. 1.3% with text

### Do Faceless Loom Videos Work?
**Mixed answer:**
- **Face on camera is generally recommended** -- it builds trust and personal connection, which drives the highest conversion lifts
- **Faceless (screen-only) CAN work** for:
  - Product demos and walkthroughs
  - Showing the prospect's website/problems
  - "Trojan Horse" strategy: lead with real value (audit their site, show missed calls data)
  - Screen recordings with voice narration
- **No published data** directly comparing faceless vs. face conversion rates
- **Recommendation:** If camera-shy, use Loom's avatar mode or Sendspark's AI approach (record once, AI personalizes)

### The "Trojan Horse" Loom Strategy (Best for AI Agencies)
1. Record a screen share of the prospect's website
2. Call their business phone and show the experience (long hold times, missed calls)
3. Demo what your AI receptionist sounds like handling the same call
4. Don't pitch -- just offer value and insights
5. End with a soft CTA: "Happy to show you more if interested"

### Loom Capture Modes
- Screen and Camera (recommended for trust)
- Screen Only (faceless)
- Camera Only
- Avatar mode (personal touch without full face)

### Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Monthly (Annual) | Monthly (Monthly) | Credits/Mo | Key Features |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | Limited | Basic search, limited emails |
| **Basic** | $49/user | $59/user | More credits | Sequences, integrations |
| **Professional** | $79/user | $99/user | Even more | Unlimited sequences, A/B testing, AI insights, lead scoring |
| **Organization** | $119/user (min 3) | $149/user (min 3) | Most | Advanced API, custom reports, international dialer |

### Credit System (Important!)
- 1 credit = 1 business email found
- 8 credits = 1 mobile phone number
- Credits expire each billing cycle
- Overage: $0.20/credit (250 minimum purchase)
- **Real cost is often 2-3x advertised price** due to credit consumption

### Key Features
- **AI Research:** Auto-generates personalized talking points per lead from LinkedIn, company news, web presence
- **AI Lead Scoring:** Predicts which leads will convert based on engagement signals
- **Meeting Intelligence:** Call recording, AI summaries, auto follow-up tasks
- **Data enrichment** across 275M+ contacts
- **Sequences** with email, calls, and tasks
- **Integrations:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, SalesLoft, LinkedIn

### API Access
- **Basic/Professional:** Very limited API access
- **Organization ($119+/user/mo, min 3 users):** Full Advanced API access
- Needed for: pushing data to custom tools, CRM integrations, custom workflows
- This is a significant cost barrier -- $357/mo minimum for API access

### Best Use for AI Agency
1. Use Apollo to build lists of companies in target industries (dental, legal, medical)
2. Filter by company size (10-200 employees), location, industry
3. Find decision-maker contacts (Office Manager, Owner, Practice Manager)
4. Enrich with emails and phone numbers
5. Export to your outreach sequences
6. Use AI Research feature to personalize messaging

## 7. Open-Source CRMs for AI Agencies

### Top Picks

#### Twenty CRM (Best Modern Option)
- **GitHub:** Most starred modern open-source CRM
- **Stack:** Modern web stack, self-hostable
- **AI:** MCP Server integration -- works with Claude for natural language CRM management
- **Best for:** Teams wanting a Salesforce alternative with full data control

#### Frappe CRM
- **GitHub:** `frappe/crm`
- **Stack:** Built on Frappe Framework (Python)
- **Features:** All-in-one lead/deal page, extensible, part of ERPNext ecosystem
- **Best for:** Teams already using or considering ERPNext

#### AI CRM Agents
- **GitHub:** `KlementMultiverse/ai-crm-agents`
- **Features:** 6 autonomous AI agents for lead qualification, email intelligence, sales pipeline, customer success, meeting scheduling, analytics
- **Best for:** AI-native agencies wanting agent-driven CRM

#### Krayin CRM
- **GitHub:** `krayin/laravel-crm`
- **Stack:** Laravel + Vue.js
- **Features:** Complete customer lifecycle, WhatsApp extension for lead gen
- **Best for:** PHP/Laravel teams

#### EspoCRM
- **GitHub:** `espocrm/espocrm`
- **Features:** Leads, contacts, opportunities, campaigns, highly customizable
- **Best for:** Traditional CRM needs with self-hosting

#### Iceburg CRM
- **GitHub:** `iceburgcrm/iceburgcrm`
- **Features:** Metadata-driven, 29 themes, AI-powered CRM creation (describe what you want, AI builds it)
- **Best for:** Rapid custom CRM setup

### Recommendation for AI Agency
**Twenty CRM** + **n8n** (for automation) is the most modern, capable stack. If you want AI agents built in, look at `ai-crm-agents`.
