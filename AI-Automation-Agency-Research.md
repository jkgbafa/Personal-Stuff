## 1. AI Audit Questions Consultants Ask During Discovery

### Phase 1: Understanding the Business
- What are your top 3-5 most time-consuming repetitive processes?
- Where does your team spend the most time on manual data entry or transfer?
- What tools/software does your team use daily? (CRM, email, project management, etc.)
- What does your current tech stack look like? Do your tools integrate with each other?
- How many hours per week does your team spend on tasks that follow a repeatable template or set of rules?
- What is your average customer value / close rate / monthly lead volume?

### Phase 2: Pain Point Identification
- Where are the biggest bottlenecks in your customer journey?
- What tasks does your team complain about most?
- How do you currently handle customer support inquiries? What percentage are repetitive/similar?
- What does your onboarding process look like (for clients or employees)?
- How do you generate and follow up with leads?
- What data do you collect but never analyze or act on?

### Phase 3: Technical Readiness Assessment
- Do your current systems have APIs available for integration?
- How is your data structured? (databases, spreadsheets, scattered documents)
- What security/compliance requirements exist in your industry?
- Do you have documented SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) for key workflows?
- Who owns the code/IP if custom solutions are built?

### Phase 4: ROI & Value Assessment
- What is the fully loaded cost (salary + overhead) of employees doing these manual tasks?
- If you could free up 10-20 hours per week of staff time, where would you redeploy that?
- What would a 20-30% improvement in response time / lead conversion be worth?
- Have you tried any AI tools or automation before? What worked or failed?

### ROI Prioritization Matrix (Use During Audit)
| Category | Action |
|---|---|
| High ROI / Low Complexity | **Quick wins** -- automate first (e.g., document classification, data entry) |
| High ROI / High Complexity | **Strategic bets** -- plan carefully (e.g., recommendation engines) |
| Low ROI / Low Complexity | Automate only if it unlocks staff time for bigger wins |
| Low ROI / High Complexity | Drop or redesign the process entirely |

**ROI Formula:** Annual ROI = (Hours Saved x Fully Loaded Cost) + (Revenue Uplift) - (AI Platform + Maintenance Costs)

## 2. Typical 90-Day AI Implementation Plan

### Phase 1: Assessment & Alignment (Days 1-30)
- **Week 1-2:** Conduct full workflow audit across People, Process, and Platforms
- **Week 2-3:** Map current-state SOPs, handoffs, and systems
- **Week 3-4:** Score and prioritize automation opportunities using the "Golden Triangle" framework (High Pain, Low Complexity, Clear ROI)
- Build cross-functional team: AI developer, business analyst, executive sponsor
- Provide basic AI literacy training for stakeholders
- Select 1-2 pilot projects to prove value
- Define success metrics (accuracy, time saved, revenue impact, NPS)

### Phase 2: Pilot Execution (Days 31-60)
- Build and deploy the selected pilot project(s)
- Integrate with existing tools and systems
- Conduct testing cycles (accuracy, consistency, speed)
- Gather user feedback and iterate
- Document everything for future scaling
- Begin measuring KPIs against baselines

### Phase 3: Scale & Governance (Days 61-90)
- Analyze pilot results and present ROI to stakeholders
- Develop scaling plan for successful automations
- Establish AI governance framework (ownership, accountability, monitoring)
- Create standard operating procedures for AI systems
- Plan next wave of automation opportunities
- Set up ongoing monitoring and optimization processes
- Transition to retainer/support engagement

### Key Success Factors
- Allocate at least **20% of budget to training and change management**
- Start with the least risky automations first (e.g., metadata, data entry before full decision-making)
- 64% of CEOs say successful AI adoption depends more on people than technology
- 30% of generative AI projects fail past proof of concept due to poor data quality (Gartner)

## 4. Frameworks & Templates for AI Business Audits

### Established Enterprise Frameworks
1. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** -- US standard for AI transparency, safety, and accountability
2. **IIA AI Auditing Framework** -- Published by the Institute of Internal Auditors; dedicated AI audit methodology
3. **COBIT** (ISACA) -- Internal controls, risk metrics, and performance measures for IT/AI governance
4. **COSO Enterprise Risk Management** -- Governance, strategy, and stakeholder collaboration for AI risk
5. **ISO 42001** -- International standard for AI management systems
6. **EU AI Act** -- Global regulatory standard (especially for "high-risk" AI systems)
7. **Colorado AI Act (SB 205)** -- First comprehensive US state framework (effective 2026)

### Consulting Firm Approaches
- **Deloitte's 4-Step AI Governance:** (1) Validate AI landscape/inventory, (2) Assess governance vs. leading practices, (3) Identify missing guardrails, (4) Test control design and effectiveness
- **EY's Internal Audit:** Structured risk assessment frameworks, inventory mapping, KPI definition, post-assessment workshops
- **PwC:** Expects end-to-end AI audit automation within 2026

### Practical Agency-Level Templates
- **3-Zone Diagnostic:** People, Process, Platforms -- examine how the team works with digital tools, bottlenecks, and system readiness
- **Workflow Scoring Matrix:** Audit 3-5 workflows, document SOPs, handoffs, and systems, define success metrics
- **Auditic Platform:** AI-powered discovery interviews, opportunity matrix (impact-effort scoring), ROI calculators with industry benchmarks, proposal generators
- **Process Street:** Free customizable audit templates for compliance, vendor management, operational reviews
- **monitorQA:** AI-powered audit template builder with auto-filling, conditional logic, multilingual checklists

### The "Audit as Sales Tool" Framework
- Charge $500-$1,500 for a standalone audit as a paid deliverable
- Some charge $5,000-$15,000 for a comprehensive 2-4 week "AI Readiness Audit"
- The audit naturally leads to $2,000-$50,000+ implementation engagements
- "Once you've done the diagnosis, you become the obvious person to build the solution"

## 5. Pricing Models for AI Consulting/Implementation

### Paid Discovery / Audit Phase
| Service | Price Range |
|---|---|
| Quick audit (standalone) | $500 -- $1,500 |
| AI Readiness Audit (2-4 weeks) | $5,000 -- $15,000 |

### Project-Based (One-Time Fees)
| Project Size | Price Range |
|---|---|
| Small pilot / MVP | $5,000 -- $40,000 |
| Medium (custom ML model + integration) | $40,000 -- $150,000 |
| Enterprise AI transformation | $100,000 -- $500,000 |
| Custom build setup fees | $2,500 -- $15,000+ |

### Monthly Retainers
| Tier | Price Range |
|---|---|
| Basic AI management & support | $2,000 -- $5,000/mo |
| Growth-focused (optimization + new builds) | $5,000 -- $15,000/mo |
| AI system support retainer | $2,000 -- $8,000/mo |
| Enterprise multi-agent management | Up to $20,000+/mo |

### Consulting / Hourly Rates
| Type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Independent consultant (hourly) | $100 -- $450/hr |
| Freelancer (daily) | $600 -- $1,200/day |
| Agency (daily, multi-disciplinary) | $1,500 -- $2,500/day |

### Performance-Based Pricing
- 20-30% of first-year documented savings
- Fee per qualified lead generated
- Percentage of revenue increase attributable to AI

### Hybrid Model (Recommended Approach)
1. Start with project-based pricing for initial traction (quick cash injection)
2. Transition clients to retainer model once value is proven (predictable recurring revenue)
3. Add usage-based fees tied to token consumption / API calls for scaling

### Key Principles
- Price based on VALUE created, not hours spent
- Focus on business impact: time saved, costs reduced, revenue increased
- The technology stack affects pricing: Make.com/Zapier solutions cost less than custom code
- Data preparation alone can cost $10K-$90K depending on complexity

## 6. Best Industries to Target for AI Automation

### Tier 1: Highest ROI / Proven Demand
1. **Healthcare & Medical** -- Patient onboarding, compliance, medical imaging, scheduling. Market projected at $28.5B+
2. **Financial Services & Banking** -- Fraud detection, KYC, risk management, compliance automation
3. **E-commerce & Retail** -- Inventory AI, recommendation engines, customer care automation
4. **SaaS Companies (High-Touch)** -- Sales automation, onboarding, managed services. Companies with $1M-$100M revenue
5. **Digital Marketing / Lead Gen Agencies** -- 80%+ of companies use AI for marketing; post scheduling, ad placement, content creation

### Tier 2: High Growth / Less Saturated
6. **Manufacturing & Supply Chain** -- 81% of manufacturers say AI is critical; predictive maintenance, quality control, inventory
7. **Legal & Professional Services** -- Document automation, compliance, contract review, smart CRMs
8. **Real Estate** -- Lead qualification, property matching, document processing
9. **Insurance** -- Claim triage, policy validation, customer communication automation

### Tier 3: Emerging / Underserved Niches
10. **Med Spas** -- $1.4-2M avg annual revenue per location, $6K+ customer LTV, desperate for modern marketing
11. **Cybersecurity** -- AI-powered security market projected at $14.9B by 2025
12. **BPO / Outsourcing** -- Reducing manual workload across support, data entry, ticket handling
13. **AI Content Repurposing** -- Turning long-form content into platform-specific assets

### Ideal Target Profile
- Businesses already spending money (revenue $500K+)
- Industries with 20-35% annual AI spend increases
- Acute pain points: labor shortages, compliance burdens, slow response times
- Digital B2B niches (easier lead sourcing than brick-and-mortar)
- Mid-market companies ($5M-$100M) -- big enough to afford, small enough to lack in-house AI teams

## Key Takeaways & Strategic Insights

1. **The audit is the entry point.** A $500-$15,000 audit naturally leads to $10K-$50K+ implementation projects. "Once you've done the diagnosis, you become the obvious person to build the solution."

2. **Niche down.** The most successful agencies specialize in one industry. Med spas, SaaS companies, healthcare, and financial services are particularly lucrative.

3. **Sell outcomes, not technology.** Clients care about cost savings, revenue growth, and efficiency -- not the complexity of your AI models.

4. **The hybrid pricing model wins.** Start with project-based for cash flow, transition to retainers for recurring revenue, add usage-based fees for scaling.

5. **The market is maturing.** By 2026, the industry is moving from "sell automation" to "sell outcomes." Agencies that offer consulting + education + software development will outcompete pure-play automation shops.

6. **Mid-market is the sweet spot.** Companies with $5M-$100M revenue are big enough to afford AI services but small enough to lack in-house AI teams.

7. **Tools are commoditizing.** The execution cost of AI is dropping ~40x per year. The real value is in business analysis, strategy, and change management -- not just building the automation.

8. **Realistic expectations:** The "$10K in 90 days" claim is marketing. A more realistic timeline is 6-12 months of intensive work to build a sustainable agency.
